Rebecca

(male)

Date of birth

09-10-1998

Date taken in

02-12-2003

Rebecca has a horrible past. She was tetoris from her nest by poachers and her mother was killed. After that she lived for years with brown bear Uli in a small concrete cage of 2m by 7m at a hotel in Albania.
She had nothing to care for and received the leftovers from the hotel as food. No water to wash herself, no tree to climb in, no grass to walk in or to dig pits, just a concrete floor full of faeces.
